A one-page newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of
**Tuesday 17 November 2026** in a single 1024×1024 image.

The lead story is Ukraine's second straight day of strikes on the Kerch
Bridge — a span of the road bridge collapsed into the sea — with Russian
missiles hitting Kyiv's bridges in reply. Around it: tankers burning near
the Strait of Hormuz and Iranian drones aimed at a US radar in Bahrain, a
third US aircraft carrier arriving in the Middle East, the first confirmed
three-way Xi–Trump–Putin meeting set for Thursday in Shenzhen, Japan
scrambling jets over an airspace breach, five European states agreeing a
joint returns facility in Rwanda, the Pentagon failing its audit for a
ninth year, and US measles cases at their highest since 1991. A footer
strip adds the Geneva killer-robot treaty talks, the Xi–Carney tariff deal,
UK sanctions on West Bank settlement builders, and the Uttarakhand bus crash.

Every word on the page is drawn programmatically, so the text is crisp and
exact; the six illustration panels are AI-generated photographs (no text in
them) laid into the composition. The finished page was checked at full size
and at 300 px across for legibility, spelling and overlaps.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png`. It needs Node 22, no network,
no dependencies, and writes identical bytes on every run.
